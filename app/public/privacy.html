Privacy Policy

Calling — last updated August 21, 2026

This policy explains what data Calling ("we", "us") collects when you play, why, and how you can control it. Calling is an independent, individually-operated project — not a registered company.

1. Who is responsible for your data

Calling. Contact for any privacy question or request: playcallingcontact@gmail.com.

2. What we collect, and why

Category What Purpose Requires consent?
Save data Your career progress, stored in your browser (local storage), never sent to our servers Let you resume a career later No — strictly necessary for the game to work
Analytics Pages viewed, in-game events (e.g. career started/completed), approximate device/location, via Google Analytics Understand how the game is played and improve it Yes
Advertising Ad interactions and identifiers used by Google AdSense to select and measure ads Show ads that fund the game's development Yes

3. Cookie consent

When you first open Calling, you're asked to accept or decline non-essential cookies. Analytics and advertising only start after you accept — nothing is loaded before that choice. If you decline, the game remains fully playable; you simply won't be tracked and won't see ads. You are not required to accept cookies to play.

4. Legal basis

Analytics and advertising cookies are processed on the basis of your consent (Article 6(1)(a) GDPR). Save-progress storage is processed as strictly necessary to provide the game itself and does not require consent.

5. Who we share data with

We use Google Analytics and Google AdSense. Google may process the data described above as an independent controller under its own terms. You can read Google's policies here:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • Google Ads Settings (to control ad personalization across Google services)

6. How long we keep data

Analytics data is retained for up to 14 months (Google Analytics' default retention period), after which it is automatically deleted. Save data stays only in your own browser's local storage until you clear it yourself or reset your browser data.

7. Your rights

Under GDPR, you can ask us to:

  • access the data we hold about you
  • correct or delete it
  • restrict or object to its processing
  • receive a copy in a portable format
  • withdraw consent at any time (this won't affect processing done before withdrawal)

To exercise any of these rights, email playcallingcontact@gmail.com. You can also lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority — in France, the CNIL.

8. Children

Calling is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them beyond the anonymous analytics/advertising described above.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the game evolves. The "last updated" date at the top will always reflect the latest version.
